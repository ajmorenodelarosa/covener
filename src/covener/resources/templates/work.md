# <title>

Checklist first, then `## ...` entries in chronological order, newest last. The engineer proposes
the design in `## Design` and waits; agents add what they did (`## Summary`, `## Decisions`, `## QA`,
`## Review`, `## Rework`); the human adds `## Feedback` with `Approved: Yes` or `Approved: No` and
the changes wanted. The last entry is the state.
